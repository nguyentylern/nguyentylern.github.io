This is a website for my research class detailing our proposed solution to a problem faced by the High Museum of Art. They had faced leaking from the skylights in the Renzo Piano buildings, and we believed that wear on the gaskets had allowed water to leak. Our solution was to replace the gaskets with new ones to prevent further leakage.

It includes video embeds and was developed with HTML and CSS.

Thank you to Ian Droessler, Hannah Lu, and Sage McGill for their contributions.
